# sh17 — Data Card

- **Full name:** SH17 (Dataset for Human Safety and PPE Detection in Manufacturing Industry)
- **Source:** paper "SH17: A Dataset for Human Safety and Personal Protective Equipment
  Detection in Manufacturing Industry" (Ahmad et al., 2025) —
  `docs/papers/07_ppe_detection/sh17_ppe_2025ahmad.pdf`, summarized at
  `docs/outputs/07_ppe_detection/sh17_ppe_2025ahmad.md`.
- **Source images:** stock photos from Pexels (see `photographer`/`url` fields in
  `raw/meta-data/*.json`), depicting manufacturing/factory-floor scenes. The paper
  describes wide-angle security-camera-style framing, 2-25m viewing distance.
- **License:** not directly confirmed in the paper as read — source images are from Pexels
  (typically free to use), but the exact license terms stated by the dataset authors have
  not been checked. Verify before using this beyond personal research.
- **Scale:** 8,099 images, 75,994 annotations, **17 classes**, grouped into 5 OSHA body
  regions (head, upper body, hands, feet, whole body) — full list in `classes.txt`
  (taken from the paper's TABLE II).
- **Heavy class imbalance:** `Person` 27.2%, `Hardhat` 15.6%, `Safety Vest` 11.7% make up
  more than half of all annotations; rare classes like `Face Shield` (0.37%),
  `Safety Harness` (0.19%), `Coveralls` (0.11%) have very few samples — low mAP for these
  classes at any small epoch count.

## Split

`raw/train_files.txt` (6,479 images) and `raw/val_files.txt` (1,620 images) are the
**author-provided split**, used to produce the benchmark numbers in the paper (TABLE III
calls the `val_files.txt` portion the "test set", an exact 80/20 ratio).

`processed/` is generated by `scripts/data/prepare_sh17.py`:
- `test` = kept as the original `val_files.txt` (1,620 images) — **directly comparable
  to the paper's reported number (70.9% mAP50 for YOLOv9-e)**.
- `train`/`val` = carved out of the original `train_files.txt`, 90/10 split, seed=42
  (train 5,831, val 648) — since the download has no separate val set for monitoring
  during training.

No junk needed cleaning from the original download (checked — no cache or duplicate
split files present).
